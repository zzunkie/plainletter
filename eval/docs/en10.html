What happens if you do not pay

1. Late payment interest is charged on overdue repayments for as long as they stay unpaid. The current rate is 6.95% a year, in addition to the interest charged on your loan balance.
2. If you do not pay or contact us, we may refer your debt to a debt collection agency in your country of residence. If you are in New Zealand, you may be stopped at the border and arrested under the Student Loan Scheme Act 2011.
3. If you cannot pay in full, you can ask for an instalment arrangement before 10 February 2027. If your circumstances have changed, you can apply to have your repayment obligation reassessed.
4. Your repayment obligation for the year ending 31 March 2027 is $2,972.00, payable in two instalments.
